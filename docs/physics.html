Physics options

Released v0.4.0 expands the WRF 4.7.1 scheme menu. Implementation, backend admission and scientific qualification are separate. Defaults and identities are frozen with F20/C5′ gates closed; the00:26Z17October registered FINAL checkpoint is66/66 accepted-scope scientific D6PASS (65full24h + c45accepted6h).

Choose an exact combination¶

The per-code support matrix covers microphysics, cumulus, PBL, surface layer, land and radiation. Each qualified row identifies its measured source and companion settings. Unbounded fields remain REPORT_ONLY; missing CPU fields make coverage incomplete.

Read the resource and science limits¶

The complete provisional E41 ledger lists every FAIL at its source. Finite output and bounded D6 do not clear F2 negatives. Jensen/NTU writers are merged; c87 scientific PASS/E41FAIL; c88 full24h scientific PASS/E41FAIL; c11 full24h PASS/E41-disclosed; NTU has an actual E41FAIL, not an unattempted scan. See known limits and the release notes.

Nested not validated¶

Where the CLI admits an option with only single-domain evidence, report nested not validated. This applies to advanced urban/lake/ocean/SSiB and PBL/cumulus paths without matched nested evidence. Actual pairing/nested refusals remain authoritative; selecting an option does not qualify it.

Historical release scope¶

The versioned v0.3.4 matrix retains its dated scope. It must not be used as the v0.4 qualification inventory.
